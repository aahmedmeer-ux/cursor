Frequently Asked Questions

What kinds of ecommerce projects does digivistaUS handle?

We work on everything from net-new ecommerce site builds to full replatforms and “rescue” projects that need to be stabilized and finished the right way. That includes B2B and B2C storefronts, dealer portals, and complex catalog builds where performance, data accuracy, and internal efficiency all matter as much as the front-end design.

Which ecommerce platforms do you work with?

Our primary ecommerce platforms are Adobe Commerce (Magento), BigCommerce, and Shopify. We help you choose the right platform based on your catalog complexity, B2B needs, integration requirements, performance goals, and internal team capacity—then design and build a solution tailored to that stack.

Can you rescue or take over an existing ecommerce project?

Yes. A large portion of our work is “rescue” and rebuild projects where a previous implementation stalled, under-delivered, or created ongoing stability and performance issues. We step in to stabilize the site, clean up data and integrations, and then move into improvements that actually support your growth and operations.

How long does an ecommerce development project usually take?

Timelines depend on the size and complexity of your project – things like number of SKUs, integrations, custom features, and design requirements. Smaller optimization or enhancement projects can be measured in weeks, while full replatforms or new builds often take several months. During discovery, we’ll define a realistic plan and timeline so expectations are clear from the beginning.

What types of businesses are a good fit for your ecommerce services?

We’re especially well suited to mid-market and enterprise merchants – often manufacturers, distributors, and parts-heavy retailers – who need more than a basic template store. If you have complex pricing, multiple customer types, heavy integrations, or operational pain tied to your ecommerce stack, you’re exactly the kind of client we typically work with. We also have a strong background in retail, and enjoy working with those clients as well.

Can you integrate our ecommerce site with ERPs, PIMs, and other systems?

Yes. Integrations are a core part of our work. We regularly connect ecommerce platforms to ERPs, PIMs, CRMs, payment gateways, shipping and tax providers, and custom internal systems. Our goal is to create a storefront that not only looks great for customers but also reduces manual work and data friction for your internal teams.

How do you approach performance and Core Web Vitals?

Performance is baked into our development approach. We focus on clean architecture, efficient theming, and smart use of caching and infrastructure so your site is fast, stable, and aligned with Core Web Vitals best practices. For existing sites, we frequently run performance audits and implement targeted improvements that meaningfully improve load times and user experience.

How do you measure success for ecommerce development projects?

Success is measured in both business and technical outcomes. On the business side, we look at revenue, conversion rate, average order value, order volume, and customer engagement. On the technical side, we track uptime, performance metrics, data accuracy, stability, and the amount of manual effort your team is able to reclaim. The goal is always the same: a site that makes your customers happier and your business more efficient.
